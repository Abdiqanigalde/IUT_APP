Why it works

Built for the way offices actually run

No more waiting in a hallway to find out an officer isn't in. Every slot you see is a real one.

Real slots, not guesses

Officers publish their actual open hours, so the time you book is the time you're seen.

Reminders that land

Email confirmations and reminders before your slot, so nothing slips through.

Live status tracking

Watch your appointment move from booked to confirmed to attended, in real time.

Priority for emergencies

Flag a genuine emergency and it's routed to the front of the queue, not lost in it.

Feedback that's used

Rate every visit — offices see the pattern and adjust, not just a star count.

Dark mode, always

Built to be easy on the eyes for the late-night booking session before a deadline.

The process

Four steps, start to finish

It's a straight line from account to appointment — no forms to chase down in person.

01

Register

03
