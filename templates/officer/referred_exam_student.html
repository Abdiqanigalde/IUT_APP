How Referred Exam Registration Works

This replaces the old paper process:

  1. Pick the courses you're sitting for the referred exam — up to 3.
  2. Submit the form below. It goes straight to the registration officer.
  3. The officer processes it and gets the required sign-offs done.
  4. You'll get a notification (and email) the moment it's ready — then just come collect it.
